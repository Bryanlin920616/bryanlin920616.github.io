## Design Context

### Users
Primary: Recruiters and hiring managers who scan quickly for signal — they want to feel Bryan is competent and likeable, not intimidated. Secondary: Tech peers and blog readers who land from links and browse at their own pace. Context: Desktop-first browsing, but mobile must hold up.

### Brand Personality
**Warm · Articulate · Unhurried**

Bryan solves complex problems clearly ("把複雜問題拆解成清晰的解法"). The portfolio should feel like that — not a flashy agency site, not a dry résumé. Like a thoughtful engineer's personal space that happens to also be beautifully designed.

Emotional goal: "I'd enjoy working with this person" and "This person has taste."

### Aesthetic Direction
**Editorial warmth** — Like a well-considered personal publication. Clean but textured. Confident type hierarchy. Warm off-white surfaces.

- Theme: Light primary, dark secondary (preserve toggle)
- Palette: Warm amber accent, cream/off-white surfaces, near-black text with warm tint
- Motion: Silky entrance animations, purposeful hovers — nothing bouncing or excessive
- Anti-references: Dark mode neon glow, rainbow gradients, excessive motion, corporate templates

### Design Principles
1. **Typography is the primary design tool** — let font size contrast create hierarchy, not boxes and borders
2. **Breathing room tells the story** — generous space between elements, nothing cramped
3. **Warmth through tint** — every neutral slightly amber-tinted; no pure gray, no pure black
4. **One accent, used sparingly** — amber only where it earns its place
5. **Motion serves communication** — staggered entrances for first load, nothing decorative
